# Privacy Policy for Headroom

Last updated: November 2026

Headroom is an open-source, evidence-based Windows memory diagnostic and management utility. This privacy policy explains our practices regarding user data.

## 1. Zero Telemetry & Local Operation
- **No Data Collection**: Headroom does not collect, record, log, or transmit any personal data, device identifiers, IP addresses, or telemetry of any kind.
- **Local Host Execution**: All performance metrics, memory diagnostics, process information, and cleaning receipts are processed strictly in-memory on your local computer and never leave your machine.
- **No External Network Calls**: Headroom contains zero analytics SDKs, zero advertising trackers, and zero background network calls.

## 2. Configuration & Receipt Storage
- Any user preferences (such as operating mode, thresholds, and process exclusion lists) and optional audit receipts are saved locally on your device in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Headroom\` or in portable mode adjacent to the executable.
- These files are completely under your control and are deleted when the application is uninstalled or manually removed.

## 3. Third-Party Services
- Headroom does not integrate with, share data with, or use any third-party telemetry, tracking, or cloud services.

## 4. Open-Source Transparency
The source code of Headroom is open source under the MIT License and publicly auditable at:
https://github.com/redwan2003-bot/HeadRoom

## 5. Contact
